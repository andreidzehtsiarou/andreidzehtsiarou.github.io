# Privacy Policy

## CleverBuy Application

**Last Updated: September 2026**

---

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy (hereinafter referred to as the "Policy") describes how Andrei Dzehtsiarou (developer and owner of the CleverBuy application, hereinafter referred to as the "Developer") collects, uses, discloses, and otherwise handles your information when you use the CleverBuy mobile application (hereinafter referred to as the "Application").

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on and with our Application.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 User-Generated Data

When you use CleverBuy, you may create and store:

- Shopping lists and items
- Shopping list history
- Notes and reminders

**All this data is stored locally on your device only.** We do NOT collect or transmit this data to external servers or third parties.

### 2.2 Technical Information Collected by the Application

When you use the Application, the following technical data is automatically collected:

- Device type and operating system version
- Application version
- App crash logs and error reports
- Usage patterns and feature interactions (which screens you visit, actions you take)

This information is collected through Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for the purpose of improving the Application and fixing bugs.

### 2.3 Data Collected by Advertising Networks

The Application displays advertisements from Google AdMob and other advertising networks. These networks automatically collect:

- **Device Identifiers:** Google Advertising ID (GAID), device model, unique device identifiers
- **Device Information:** Operating system version, device language, device type
- **Usage Data:** Your interactions with the Application (screens viewed, features used, time spent in app, clicks)
- **Location Data:** Approximate location (coarse/network-based location) if location permissions are granted
- **Crash and Performance Data:** App crashes, performance metrics, error reports
- **IP Address and Network Information:** For analytics and fraud detection

---

## 3. Data Storage and Security

### 3.1 Your Personal Content (Shopping Lists, History, Notes)

**Local Storage:** All your shopping lists, history, and personal content are stored locally on your Android device using the device's secure storage system. Only you have access to this data on your device.

**No Cloud Storage:** We do NOT use cloud services to store your personal shopping data.

**No Transmission to Third Parties:** Your shopping lists and personal information are NEVER transmitted to our servers, advertising networks, or other third parties.

### 3.2 Technical Data and Usage Information

**Stored:** Technical data (crash logs, analytics) is transmitted to Google Firebase and Google Analytics servers for analysis and debugging.

**Encrypted Transmission:** All data is transmitted through secure HTTPS connections with encryption.

**Data Retention:** Firebase and Google Analytics retain this data in accordance with their privacy policies. Typically, this data is retained for analysis purposes and may be automatically deleted after certain periods.

---

## 4. How Your Data Is Used

### 4.1 Data Used by the Developer

Technical information (crash reports, usage patterns, performance data) is used by the Developer to:

- Identify and fix bugs and errors
- Monitor application stability and performance
- Understand how users interact with features
- Improve the Application's functionality and user experience
- Ensure security and prevent fraud

### 4.2 Data Used by Advertising Networks (Google AdMob)

The Application uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob and other advertising networks use the data they collect for:

- **Displaying Targeted Advertisements:** Showing ads tailored to your interests based on your device type, usage patterns, and location
- **Measuring Ad Performance:** Tracking clicks, impressions, and conversions to measure advertising effectiveness
- **Analytics and Analytics Services:** Understanding aggregate trends in app usage and ad performance
- **Fraud Prevention and Security:** Detecting and preventing fraudulent activity
- **Personalization:** Tailoring ad content based on your interaction history with the Application

**Important:** Advertising networks operate under their own privacy policies and may use your data for purposes beyond the Developer's control or knowledge.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

### 5.1 Advertising Networks

The Application integrates Google AdMob and other advertising networks. These services collect and process data as described in Section 4.2.

**What the Developer Does NOT Share:**
- ❌ We do NOT manually share your shopping lists with advertisers
- ❌ We do NOT share your personal content with advertising networks
- ❌ We do NOT sell your personal shopping data to third parties

**What Advertising Networks Collect Automatically:**
- ✅ Device identifiers and device information
- ✅ Application usage data and interaction patterns
- ✅ Approximate location (if permissions granted)
- ✅ Crash reports and app performance data

These services collect this data automatically through their SDKs integrated into the Application.

**Privacy Policies:**
- [Google AdMob Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Google Analytics Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### 5.2 Google Play Services

The Application uses Google Play Services for:

- App distribution and updates
- Crash reporting and analytics (Firebase Crashlytics)
- Performance monitoring

Google may collect data in accordance with [Google Play Services Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### 5.3 Firebase

The Application uses Google Firebase for:

- Crash reporting and error tracking
- App analytics and performance monitoring

Data collected by Firebase is used to improve the Application. See [Firebase Privacy & Security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

---

## 6. Data Subject Rights and User Control

You have the following rights and control over your data:

### 6.1 Your Shopping Data

- **Delete All Data:** Uninstall the Application to delete all your shopping lists and local data from your device
- **Clear Application Data:** Use your Android device settings to clear the Application's local storage
- **No Account Required:** We don't require user registration or accounts, so there's no personal profile to manage

### 6.2 Advertising and Analytics Data

- **Limit Ad Tracking:** On your Android device, you can reset your Google Advertising ID or opt-out of personalized ads in Google Settings
- **Manage Location Services:** Disable location permissions for the Application in your device settings
- **Google Account Controls:** You can manage your data in your Google Account settings and disable certain tracking features

### 6.3 Data Deletion Request

If you wish to request deletion of your data or have questions about data processing, please contact the Developer at the email provided in Section 9.

---

## 7. Data Retention

### 7.1 Local Data (Shopping Lists, History)

Stored on your device until you delete the Application or manually clear the Application's data.

### 7.2 Crash Reports and Analytics

- **Firebase Crashlytics:** Retains data for debugging and analysis; typically retained for 90 days or longer depending on configuration
- **Google Analytics:** Retains data according to its retention settings (typically 14-26 months)

### 7.3 Advertising Data

Google AdMob and other advertising networks retain data in accordance with their own privacy policies and retention schedules.
