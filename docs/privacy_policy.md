# Privacy Policy

## CleverBuy Application

**Last Updated: August 2024**

---

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy (hereinafter referred to as the "Policy") describes how Andrei Dzehtsiarou (developer and owner of the CleverBuy application, hereinafter referred to as the "Developer") collects, uses, discloses, and otherwise handles your information when you use the CleverBuy mobile application (hereinafter referred to as the "Application").

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on and with our Application.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 User-Generated Data

When you use CleverBuy, you may create and store:

- Shopping lists and items
- Shopping list history
- Notes and reminders

**All this data is stored locally on your device only.** We do NOT collect or transmit this data to external servers.

### 2.2 Technical Information

When you use the Application, we may automatically receive:

- Device type and operating system version
- App version and crash logs (for error reporting)
- Usage patterns and feature interactions

This information is collected for the purpose of improving the Application and is NOT linked to your personal identity.

---

## 3. Data Storage and Security

**Local Storage:** All your shopping lists, history, and personal data are stored locally on your Android device using the device's secure storage system. Only you have access to this data on your device.

**No Cloud Storage:** We do NOT use cloud services to store your personal data.

**No Server Transmission:** Your shopping lists and personal information are NEVER transmitted to our servers or third parties.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

### 4.1 Advertising Networks

The Application may display advertisements from Google AdMob and other advertising networks. These networks may:

- Use cookies or similar tracking technologies
- Collect data about your interaction with advertisements
- Build aggregated profiles about your interests

However, **we do NOT share your personal data from the Application with these advertising networks.**

Each advertising network operates under its own privacy policy:
- [Google AdMob Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### 4.2 Google Play Services

The Application may use Google Play Services for:
- App distribution and updates
- In-app purchases (if implemented)
- Crash reporting and analytics

---

## 5. What We Do NOT Do

❌ **We do NOT:**

- Sell or trade your personal data to anyone
- Share your shopping lists with third parties
- Send your data to external servers without explicit consent
- Use your data for targeted advertising based on your shopping behavior
- Store your personal data in cloud services
- Create user profiles or accounts with external services
- Collect location data
- Collect contact information or personal identifiers

---

## 6. Your Rights and Control

You have complete control over your data:

- **Delete the App:** Delete all your data by uninstalling the Application
- **Clear Local Data:** Use your device settings to clear the Application's local storage
- **No Account Needed:** We don't require user registration or accounts

---
