# 🛒 CleverBuy

**Smart Shopping List Application** for Android

[← Back to Portfolio](/)

## About CleverBuy

CleverBuy is a minimalist, efficient shopping list application that helps you organize your purchases, get notifications, and keep track of your shopping history.

### Features

- ✅ Create and manage shopping lists
- 🔔 Get push notifications for your lists
- 📋 View shopping history
- 🎯 Simple and intuitive interface
- 🎨 Material You design

### Technologies Used

- **Language:** Kotlin
- **UI:** Jetpack Compose
- **Architecture:** MVVM
- **Local Storage:** Room
- **Dependency Injection:** Koin
- **Notifications:** Android FCM
- **Design:** Material You
