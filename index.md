# 🛒 CleverBuy

**Smart Shopping List Application** for Android

## About CleverBuy

CleverBuy is a simple and efficient shopping list application that helps you organize your purchases, get notifications, and keep track of your shopping history.

### Features

- ✅ Create and manage shopping lists
- 🔔 Get notifications for your lists
- 📋 View shopping history
- 🎯 Simple and intuitive interface
